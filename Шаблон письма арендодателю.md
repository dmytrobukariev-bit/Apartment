# Шаблоны письма арендодателю

Перед отправкой замени всё в [квадратных скобках]. Строки, которые к тебе
не относятся (например, про гаранта или работу), просто удали.

## English (полная версия: e-mail, Pararius, Funda)

**Subject:** Rental inquiry – [address of the property]

Dear [name of landlord / Sir or Madam],

I am very interested in the [studio / apartment] at [address], listed on [website].

My name is [Name], I am [age] years old and I am a [Bachelor/Master] student of
[programme] at Wittenborg University of Applied Sciences here in Apeldoorn. I am a
quiet, tidy and reliable tenant, I do not smoke and I have no pets.

I am looking for a home from **11 January 2027**, and I can also move in a little
earlier if that suits you better.

I can provide the following documents right away:
- copy of my passport / residence permit
- proof of enrolment at Wittenborg
- bank statements / proof of income or scholarship
- a guarantor ([my parents]) with proof of their income
- [an employment contract]

The possibility to register at the address (inschrijving BRP) is important for me.
Could you confirm that this is possible?

I would be happy to schedule a viewing at any time that suits you, in person or by
video call. You can reach me by phone or WhatsApp at [+31 6 ...].

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,
[Name]
[phone] · [e-mail]

## Если звонишь по телефону (на английском)

> Hello, my name is [Name]. I'm calling about the [studio/apartment] at [address].
> Is it still available? I'm a student at Wittenborg here in Apeldoorn and I'm
> looking to move in from around the 11th of January. Would it be possible to come
> for a viewing? And is registration at the address possible?

---

## Полезные фразы
- *Is the apartment still available?* / *Is de woning nog beschikbaar?*: квартира ещё свободна?
- *Is registration possible?* / *Is inschrijving mogelijk?*: можно зарегистрироваться?
- *What is included in the rent?* / *Wat is inbegrepen in de huur?*: что входит в аренду?
- *How much is the deposit?* / *Hoe hoog is de borg?*: какой депозит?
- *When can I come for a viewing?* / *Wanneer kan ik komen kijken?*: когда можно посмотреть?
